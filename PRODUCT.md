## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers filling full-time remote roles. They reach the site while evaluating candidates and decide whether Alex is worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Alex Gutierrez. Success means a hiring visitor understands what kind of engineer he is and what he has built, then gets in touch or downloads his CV.

## Positioning

Frontend-leaning engineer with full-stack depth: strong on UI, and also carries backend and deployment. Known for building products from scratch and for work involving maps and 3D. From the work history and projects:

- Babooni Technologies (Feb 2026 to present): designed and built from scratch a CRM/ERP platform for a telecommunications company.
- Oreka IT (May 2025 to Feb 2026, Full-Stack Developer and Technical Owner): designed and developed from scratch the water meter reading application for AMVISA, Vitoria-Gasteiz's public municipal water company.
- GESTIOMA (Dec 2022 to May 2025): contributed to the company's main product, a 3D model viewer, adding 360° images, GeoJSON maps and traffic cameras, and redesigning its 2.0 interface.
- BuyCarMap (personal project): second-hand car listings from three Spanish marketplaces merged into one map-based search.

## Operating Context

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: plain and specific. Concrete facts over buzzwords, in the register of the existing BuyCarMap and Simplest Markdown descriptions. Humour lives in the robot, not in the main copy.
- The "clanker" 3D robot and its dev-humour lines stay.
- Red #c2181d and the KH Teka typeface are part of the identity. The red was darkened from #ff4b44 on 2026-09-11 to pass WCAG AA contrast.
